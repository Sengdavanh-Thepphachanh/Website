Research project · 2023–2028

CLANCY

Chinese mitten crab control

The project develops and tests traps for invasive Chinese mitten crabs. TU Dresden investigates how crabs respond to currents and hydraulic barriers.

Institution
Chair of Hydraulic Engineering, TU Dresden
Funding
EU Interreg North Sea
Visit the project at TU Dresden Research project · 2025–2027

BIBOB

Beaver dams and water management

The project studies beaver dams and their effects on water retention. The team develops practical dam management guidance for the Saxon-Czech border region.

Institution
Chair of Hydraulic Engineering, TU Dresden
Funding
EU Interreg Saxony–Czech Republic
