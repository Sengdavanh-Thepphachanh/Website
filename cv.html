Profile

Scientific researcher at TU Dresden, from Laos and now based in Dresden. Research focuses on river habitat modelling and hydraulic processes in freshwater ecosystems.

Research

CLANCY · Chinese mitten crab control

2023–2028 · EU Interreg North Sea

The project develops and tests traps for invasive Chinese mitten crabs. TU Dresden investigates how crabs respond to currents and hydraulic barriers. Project record

BIBOB · Beaver dams and water management

2025–2027 · EU Interreg Saxony–Czech Republic

The project studies beaver dams and their effects on water retention. The team develops practical dam management guidance for the Saxon-Czech border region. Project record
